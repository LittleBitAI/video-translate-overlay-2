# Fit the entire introduction site into a single four-column grid, and include the synchronization capture without cropping.

What. Three pages use one four-column grid (`--cols`, one `--gap` gap). The reading text is two columns, the mobile vertical capture is the third column, the four steps are one column each, the three numbers are the first to third columns, and the measurement evidence is the fourth column. The usage table, the two installation platforms, and the front and back chapters are also placed on the same line. - The synchronization capture is included in its original ratio without cropping the original. The Windows window is three columns, and the Android vertical screen is the fourth column. - DESIGN. …

Why. The column division was different for each section. The numbers were three columns, the storage box right below was 1 : 1.25, and the installation method was two columns again, so the lines of the sections above and below did not align at all. In the device sharing section, the vertical Android screen was cut into a horizontal strip, making it look different from the actual screen.
